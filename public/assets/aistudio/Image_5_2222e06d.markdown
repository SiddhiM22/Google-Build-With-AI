## 1. Visual Theme and Atmosphere
The app feels like an illustrated pocket travel zine or indie comic strip. Configured in a clean light mode atmosphere, it utilizes **airy, muted pastels** against a crisp light paper canvas (`#232326` accents). Every screen is structured like a clean storyboard: distinct content blocks sit inside crisp `2px` ink-bordered panels (`#232326`) with solid, offset comic drop shadows (`3px 3px 0px #232326`). 
The layout prioritizes **speed and low-friction planning**. Keep screens focused on the core itinerary, map route, and essential transit details without promotional banners, aggressive upsells, or cluttered multi-step carousels.

## 2. Color Palette and Roles
- **Canvas & Surface**: Used for clean light mode backgrounds and subtle map/timeline tracks so pastel cards stand out clearly without eye strain.
- **Primary Action (`#FFB5A7` Pastel Coral)**: Reserved strictly for primary buttons (e.g., `+ Add Stop`, `Save Trip`) and the active day selector. Always pair with dark ink text to maintain high WCAG contrast.
- **Semantic Category Tints**:
  - **Transit & Flights (`#A8DADC` Powder Blue)**: Used for flight tickets, train legs, and route connectors.
  - **Places & Stays (`#FDE4CF` Buttercream)**: Used for hotel cards, food spots, and sightseeing panels.
  - **Confirmed & Budget (`#CDEAC0` Sage Mint)**: Used for verified reservations, packing checkmarks, and remaining budget indicators.
- **Comic Ink**: Used for all text, icons, panel outlines, and hard offset shadows, calibrated for light mode readability.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display & Panel Headers**: Use a geometric, slightly rounded grotesque (`Space Grotesk`) in Bold (`700`) to echo hand-lettered comic titles while remaining crisp on mobile screens.
- **Body & Itinerary Details**: Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` at `15px` with generous line height (`22px`) for scannable stop descriptions and addresses.
- **Timestamps, Flight Codes & Prices**: Use `Space Mono` (`12px`–`14px`, uppercase) inside small pill badges to give tickets and schedules a stamped passport feel.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Itinerary Panel Cards**: Clean surface background, `2px solid #232326` border, `14px` corner radius, and `3px 3px 0px #232326` hard shadow. Include a pastel header strip or corner sticker badge indicating the time of day.
- **Primary Pill Buttons**: Full pill shape (`999px` radius), `#FFB5A7` fill, `2px solid #232326` border, bold uppercase label, and `3px 3px 0px #232326` shadow that shifts to `1px 1px 0px` on tap.
- **Speech-Bubble Tips & Notes**: Use subtle tail pointers on user notes or local travel tips with `#FDE4CF` fill and `1.5px solid #232326` borders.
- **Day-by-Day Storyboard Timeline**: Connect sequential itinerary cards vertically using a dashed `#232326` ink line (`2px dashed`) with small circular pastel nodes representing transit duration between stops.
- **Custom Map View**: Style map tiles in light tones, soft sage parks (`#CDEAC0`), and powder blue water (`#A8DADC`), with numbered comic-pin markers.

## 5. Layout Principles & Anti-Patterns (Don'ts)
- **Comic Grid Rhythm**: Arrange summary stats (Budget, Days, Stops) in asymmetric 2-column comic grid panels separated by `12px` gutters.
- **Do NOT use soft blurred drop shadows**: All elevation must come from crisp, zero-blur hard offset shadows (`3px 3px 0px #232326`).
- **Do NOT use neon or high-saturation primaries**: Keep all fills strictly within the desaturated pastel palette.
- **Do NOT add clutter**: Avoid pop-up cross-sells, dense nested carousels, or redundant confirmation modals during trip creation.
